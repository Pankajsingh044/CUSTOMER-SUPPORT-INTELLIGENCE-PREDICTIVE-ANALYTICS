Rubric Criterion 1: Data Preparation

Accurate Data Preparation & Feature Pipeline

Addressing lifecycle missingness and engineering actionable operational signals.

1. Business Lifecycle Missingness Handling

• Diagnosis: Open (2,819) and Pending (2,881) tickets naturally lack closure timestamps and customer satisfaction ratings because cases remain unresolved.
• Methodology: Rather than naive mean-filling (which corrupts target labels), predictive modeling isolates verified closed tickets (2,769) for ground-truth evaluation.
• Imputation: Duration deltas are computed from timestamps; negative anomalies and open queues are imputed using robust domain median ($6.34\text{ hours}$).

2. Engineered Feature Transformations

• Response_Time_Hours: Elapsed hours from ticket creation to first agent touchpoint.
• Resolution_Time_Hours: Elapsed duration from first response to final ticket resolution.
• Priority_Score: Ordinal urgency integer ($1 = \text{Low}$ to $4 = \text{Critical}$).
• Escalated: Operational flag for Critical/High priority inquiries.
• Customer_Age_Group: Cohorts (<25 Gen Z, 25-39 Millennial, 40-54 Gen X, 55+ Boomer).
• Desc_Length: Character count measuring customer problem complexity.
